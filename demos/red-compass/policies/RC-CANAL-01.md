# The Invisible Extension Test

Document: RC-CANAL-01 · Version: 1 · Corpus: demo-2026-01

## 8.1 Reflected silhouette

On a parcel beside a listed canal, a proposal changing the silhouette visible in canal reflection counts as an extension even if invisible from the road. Ordinary new roofs or raised walls change the silhouette unless the proposal establishes unchanged outward profile. Consult RC-REV-01 and RC-BASE-01.

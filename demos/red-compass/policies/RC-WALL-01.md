# The Listening Wall Ordinance

Document: RC-WALL-01 · Version: 1 · Corpus: demo-2026-01

## 2.1 The unheard frontage

Any wall facing an archived bell route is frontage even if the applicant calls it a rear garden wall. Consult parcel records. New or heightened frontage walls are prohibited. Repairs at unchanged height and footprint may proceed subject to other policies.

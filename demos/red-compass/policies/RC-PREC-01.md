# Precedent: The LED Lantern

Document: RC-PREC-01 · Version: 1 · Corpus: demo-2026-01

## 13.1 Approved illustration

A protected public evening lantern with unchanged stone exterior, outward form and footprint, receiving a hidden LED replacement, was approved under RC-LAN-01 after the register confirmed no habitat. The same proposal during a registered nesting closure would be denied.

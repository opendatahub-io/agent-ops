# The Former Use Definition

Document: RC-USE-01 · Version: 1 · Corpus: demo-2026-01

## 10.1 Function precedes geometry

The former use is the function in the authoritative heritage record, or the existing use stated consistently in the proposal for unprotected parcels. A ceremonial stair becoming everyday access, a lantern becoming an advertising sign, or a garden becoming a commercial terrace changes use. Unchanged dimensions alone do not preserve use.

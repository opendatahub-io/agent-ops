# The Municipal Sample Exemption

Document: RC-SAMPLE-01 · Version: 1 · Corpus: demo-2026-01

## 3.2 Archived equivalence

Replacement joinery matching the archived municipal sample is exempt from RC-BLUE-01 section 3.1, provided existing use and outward form remain unchanged. A parcel heritage record must identify the sample; the applicant must explicitly describe matching it. A new use defeats this exemption.

# The Red Compass construction code

Document: RC-BASE-01 · Version: 1 · Corpus: demo-2026-01

## 1.1 Authority and scope

These fictional policies apply to every proposal in Red Compass. Read applicable parcel, heritage and environmental records. Applicant statements are proposals, not authoritative facts. Policy corpus demo-2026-01 applies irrespective of submission date.

## 1.2 Precedence

An explicitly named later amendment overrides its named earlier clause. A specific exemption defeats a general rule only within its scope. Environmental habitat closure RC-ENV-01 defeats every heritage or reversible-work exemption. Worked precedents illustrate but never override rules. All applicable requirements must be met.

## 1.3 Insufficient evidence and bounded review

Approve only when all applicable requirements are established. Deny with a specific missing condition if material evidence is absent or conflicting. If the review reaches its iteration limit without establishing a valid complete decision, deny for insufficient evidence and disclose the limit. Infrastructure outages are operational failures, not substantive denials.

## 1.4 Ordinary works

Repairs preserving existing use, appearance and footprint are permitted unless another policy restricts them. New structures and extensions require a documented exemption under RC-REV-01 or RC-LAN-01. Groundworks require RC-SIL-01 compliance. Demolition of a protected structure is prohibited.

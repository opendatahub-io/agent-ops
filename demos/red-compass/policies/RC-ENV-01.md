## 7.1 Habitat closure

Later override of RC-LAN-01, RC-REV-01 and RC-DRAIN-01: on parcels with registered habitat, any exterior construction or groundworks overlapping the habitat closed dates is prohibited. Dates are inclusive. Purely internal work requires explicit evidence it cannot disturb the exterior habitat.

# The Third Silence Restriction

Document: RC-SIL-01 · Version: 1 · Corpus: demo-2026-01

## 5.1 Observance dates

Groundworks overlapping the third local silence observance are prohibited. Consult environmental records for inclusive start and end dates. RC-DRAIN-01 provides the sole observance exemption. Work outside these dates is not restricted by this clause.

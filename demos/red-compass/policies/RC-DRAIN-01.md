## 5.2 Narrow emergency exception

RC-SIL-01 section 5.1 does not bar emergency repair of an existing failed drain if the proposal identifies an immediate flooding failure, creates no new permanent footprint, and preserves drainage direction. New drainage capacity for future development is not an emergency. RC-ENV-01 remains binding.

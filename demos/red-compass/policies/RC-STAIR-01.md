# The Dormant Stair Exemption

Document: RC-STAIR-01 · Version: 1 · Corpus: demo-2026-01

## 4.1 Dormant repairs

A ceremonial stair may be repaired within its existing footprint and historic function. Restoring it for everyday access changes its function and defeats the repair exemption, even when no dimensions change. Consult RC-USE-01.
